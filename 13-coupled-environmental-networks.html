Complex biological communities do not function in isolation; they respond to environmental drivers, clinical phenotypes, and host tissue states. cliplot_env_network() integrates a central co-occurrence network with peripheral correlation heatmaps across 7 styling presets.

13.1 Architecture and Visual Coordinate System

The layout integrates three distinct analytical layers on a unified coordinate system conforming to Fig4papers publication rules:

  1. Central Modular Network (r \le 2.80): Displays functional modules with radial exclusion boundaries and bold opaque badges (M1: Core, M2: Barrier, etc.) eliminating text-stroke collisions.
  2. Four Corner Triangular Heatmaps: Displays pairwise intra-domain correlations with significance asterisks (*P < 0.05, **P < 0.01, ***P < 0.001) and 78° angled outer labels. Diagonal self-correlations (r = 1.0) remain unstarred.
  3. Annular Inter-Domain Connecting Links: Originates from indicator badges in the annular corridor (r \approx 3.38) and connects to the central network perimeter (r \approx 2.74). Color indicates direction (red for positive, cyan for negative) and linewidth indicates statistical significance (-\log_{10}P).

Code 
library(cliomicplot)

p_env_clin <- cliplot_env_network(
  title              = "Central Microbial Network Coupled with Multi-Domain Clinical Drivers",
  show_values        = TRUE,
  show_domain_labels = TRUE,
  value_digits       = 2,
  compact            = TRUE,
  preset             = "nature",
  scifig             = TRUE
)

# Central Networks Coupled with Multi-Domain Environmental Heatmaps

Complex biological communities do not function in isolation; they respond to environmental drivers, clinical phenotypes, and host tissue states. `cliplot_env_network()` integrates a central co-occurrence network with peripheral correlation heatmaps across 7 styling presets.

## Architecture and Visual Coordinate System

The layout integrates three distinct analytical layers on a unified coordinate system conforming to Fig4papers publication rules:

1. **Central Modular Network ($r \le 2.80$)**: Displays functional modules with radial exclusion boundaries and bold opaque badges (`M1: Core`, `M2: Barrier`, etc.) eliminating text-stroke collisions.
2. **Four Corner Triangular Heatmaps**: Displays pairwise intra-domain correlations with significance asterisks ($*P < 0.05$, $**P < 0.01$, $***P < 0.001$) and 78° angled outer labels. Diagonal self-correlations ($r = 1.0$) remain unstarred.
3. **Annular Inter-Domain Connecting Links**: Originates from indicator badges in the annular corridor ($r \approx 3.38$) and connects to the central network perimeter ($r \approx 2.74$). Color indicates direction (red for positive, cyan for negative) and linewidth indicates statistical significance ($-\log_{10}P$).

## The 7 Environmental Coupling Styles

| Preset | Central Network | Peripheral Domains | Palette Aesthetics |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| `clinical` / `nature`| Multi-module microbiome | Biophysical, Severity, Cytokines, Exposome | Nature Slate Blue, Crimson, Amber |
| `seasonal` | Seasonal succession | Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter | Flora Green, Solar Amber, Rust, Frost Blue |
| `dark` | Luminescent multi-omics| 4 Multi-omics layers | Obsidian background, neon cyan & magenta |
| `lancet` | Clinical epidemiology | Clinical trials exposome | Muted greys, thin editorial strokes |
| `multi_tissue` | Multi-tissue axis | Epidermis, Dermis, Serum, Gut | Terracotta, Cerulean, Amethyst, Forest |
| `circadian` | Diurnal oscillations | Dawn (06:00), Midday (12:00), Dusk (18:00), Midnight (24:00) | Dawn Rose, Noon Gold, Dusk Indigo, Midnight Navy |
| `bipolar` | Differential coupling | Healthy vs Disease contrasts | Polar divergent Crimson vs Deep Blue |

### Style 1: Clinical Phenotype Domains (Nature Style)

library(cliomicplot)

p_env_clin <- cliplot_env_network(
  title              = "Central Microbial Network Coupled with Multi-Domain Clinical Drivers",
  show_values        = TRUE,
  show_domain_labels = TRUE,
  value_digits       = 2,
  compact            = TRUE,
  preset             = "nature",
  scifig             = TRUE
